Hey, I'm Rinkesh.

I'm a product builder and two-time founder. My first company, Savior, ran daily operations for 85 hospitals before Zocdoc bought it in 2020. Then I built Career Leap from a WhatsApp group into a learning platform used by 10,000 people. It grew, but the business didn’t work at scale, so I shut it down. Now I’m building again — in the open, mostly with AI.

The part I like most is the very beginning, when the problem is still messy and nobody knows yet if it'll work.

Away from the screen I'm chasing the city's best filter coffee, losing at board games I picked myself, and letting Simba supervise the rest.

What I'm looking for

The earliest stage — a rough problem, no product yet, still figuring out if it's even real. I like being close to users, shipping fast, and being wrong quickly. Founder, founding PM, or a 0→1 lead.
