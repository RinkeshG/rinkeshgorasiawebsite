Hi, I'm Rinkesh.

Product & growth leader, and a two-time founder.

I started my first company, Savior, in healthcare while still in college. What began as a side project grew into a platform used by 85+ leading hospitals across India and was eventually acquired by Zocdoc in 2021.

I then built Career Leap, an edtech platform where we spent 3 years experimenting, pivoting, and scaling to 10,000 users with early revenue before shutting it down when the unit economics didn't hold at scale.

Since then, I've worked closely with founders as a product and growth partner, helping them build, scale, and raise.

Lately, I'm back to building. Exploring AI through projects like KnowYourPay and Product Sense Lab.

Open to building the next 0→1 and beyond.

I work best in small, high-ownership teams where I can own product and growth end-to-end. Early or growth-stage.
