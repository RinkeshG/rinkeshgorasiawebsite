The Ledger.

The site is a document, not a screen — an operator's record, warmly typeset. Everything below is the whole system; if a new element can't cite one of these rules, it doesn't ship.

Principles
01 · Two voicesHanken Grotesk is the words. Spline Sans Mono is the data — dates, figures, domains, labels of record. Nothing else, ever.
02 · Red is reservedRed marks status, verdicts, stamps and focus. It is never decoration. One red idea per region of the page.
03 · Flat, except evidenceThe record is flat. Only artifacts from outside the document — live product screenshots — get depth (shadow, lift).
04 · Rules draw structureA 2px ink rule opens a section. A 1px hairline divides entries. No boxes, no cards, no fills to fake hierarchy.
05 · Recompose for mobileColumns become stacked tallies; gutters become inline labels. Never shrink the desktop layout.
06 · Every word is auditedMeta text exists only if a visitor acts on it. No "est. 2017", no read times, no section counts.
Colour

White with a breath of warm grey, plus a 5% grain overlay (SVG noise, baked into ledger.css) — paper tooth, not paper colour.

Motion
The red threadThe margin line inks in as far as you've read. Scroll-linked, 1px, desktop only.
TallyFigures count up over 700ms (cubic ease-out) when they enter view. Once.
Stamp press350ms press on row hover — scale 1.14, rotate −3.5°, settle.
Arrow slideIndex arrow fades in and slides 4px on hover. Always visible on touch.
SimbaPortrait hover/tap pops a mono speech bubble; the line rotates. FIG. 01 stays deadpan.
DisciplineDurations ≤ 700ms. Nothing loops except the status pulse. prefers-reduced-motion kills everything.
Never
  • A third typeface, or mono set as prose.
  • Red used decoratively, or two red ideas in one region.
  • Cards, pills, or filled boxes to group content — rules only.
  • Meta text nobody acts on (counts, read times, "est." lines).
  • Shadows on anything that isn't evidence from a live product.
  • Shrinking the desktop grid to fit a phone.
