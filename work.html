The work.

Two companies I founded, two years backing founders, and what I’m building now. One sold, one I shut down myself — here’s both, honestly.

Savior

Founder & CEO · Healthcare
2017–2020Acquired by Zocdoc

Healthcare software that ran daily operations for 85 hospitals across India — profitable on ₹0 raised, and acquired by Zocdoc.

  • Started as a booking app. Weeks spent inside hospitals showed the real problem was the data, not booking — so I rebuilt it to run the whole operation, with first pilots in three months.
  • It solved painful daily work, so it grew word of mouth: 35 → 85 hospitals, no sales team, chains like Fortis and Cloudnine. A team of 85, a second version with analytics, profitable the whole way.
  • Acquired by Zocdoc in 2020 — never having raised a rupee.

2017–2020  ·  85 hospitals  ·  ₹0 raised  ·  profitable  ·  acquired

Career Leap

Founder & CEO · Learning
2020–2023Shut down, my call

A WhatsApp group that became a learning company — it grew fast and looked healthy. I shut it down myself.

  • 10,000 users in six months, then a learning app that crossed 100k watch hours and doubled retention.
  • Live cohorts hit 40% completion where the category sees 5–10% — because I built for the last lesson, not the signup. ₹15L revenue across two cohorts.
  • Every engagement metric worked; the unit economics never did, and no feature was going to change that math. So I closed it while I still respected the work — and I’d make the same call again.

2020–2023  ·  10K users  ·  40% completion  ·  ₹15L revenue  ·  shut down
