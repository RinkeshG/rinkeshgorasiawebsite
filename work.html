companies

Savior

2017-2020

started in college as software to run one hospital's out-patient desk; grew into a platform 85+ hospitals used to manage daily operations and preventive care. the real work was sitting inside hospitals until i understood the gap between how care is supposed to run and how it actually does. that gap was the product. i sold it, onboarded the first 30 clients myself, and ran a team of 8.

85+ hospitals  ·  ~$1M ARR  ·  acquired by Zocdoc, 2020

C

Career Leap

2020-2023

started as a WhatsApp group for students; reached 10,000 people in six months on word of mouth. we shipped a video-course app, grew a 3,500-member community with three people, and hit 40% completion on live cohorts (industry average is 5-10%). then growth got too expensive to keep going, so i shut it down.

10K users in 6mo  ·  40% completion  ·  ₹15L revenue
